Free · Windows 10 and 11 · Built with Meta Muse

It notices pain points before you.

It sees where your hours go and what you keep redoing. Every number is real.

It suggests small apps, and says why.

Change an idea, or say no. It learns either way.

It builds the app, then tries it like you would.

Problems it finds go back to be fixed before you ever see them.

Tell it what you're working toward.

Write a goal in your own words. Progress is counted, and ideas aim at it.

Then it's simply an app on your PC.

Its own window, icon and Start menu entry. Offline unless you say otherwise.

Four Meta Muse agents. One job each.

  1. ObserverMuse SparkFinds what you repeat
  2. DesignerMuse Spark + WonderTurns it into ideas and plans
  3. BuilderMuse WonderWrites and fixes the app
  4. TesterMuse SparkUses it like you would

Your computer, your call.

  • Learning stays off until you turn it on.
  • Never what you type or copy. No passwords, banking or private chats.
  • Apps see only what you approve, and never the internet at the same time.

Make your computer fit you.
